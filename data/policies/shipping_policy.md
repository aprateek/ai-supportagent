# ShopSmart Shipping Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

## Shipping Options

| Method | Delivery Time | Cost |
|--------|--------------|------|
| Standard Shipping | 5–7 business days | Free on orders over $35; otherwise $5.99 |
| Expedited Shipping | 2–3 business days | $12.99 |
| Next-Day Shipping | 1 business day | $24.99 |

## Order Processing

Orders placed before **2:00 PM EST** on business days are processed the same day.

## Tracking

A tracking number is emailed within 24 hours of shipment.

## International Shipping

ShopSmart currently ships to the **United States and Canada** only. International delivery typically takes **7–14 business days**.

## Lost or Damaged Shipments

If your package is lost or arrives damaged, contact us within **48 hours** of the expected delivery date.

For shipping inquiries, contact **support@shopsmart.com** or call **1-800-SHOP-SMART**.
