# ShopSmart Return Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

## Return Window

All items purchased from ShopSmart may be returned within **30 days** of the delivery date. Items returned after 30 days are not eligible for a refund or exchange.

## Conditions for Return

To qualify for a return, items must meet the following conditions:

- The item must be **unused and in its original packaging**, including all tags, accessories, and documentation.
- Proof of purchase (order confirmation email or receipt) is required.
- Items must be free of damage, stains, or signs of wear.

## Refund Timeline

Once we receive and inspect your return, refunds are processed within **5–7 business days**. Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to a ShopSmart error.

## Exceptions

The following items are **not eligible** for return:

- Gift cards and digital downloads
- Perishable goods (food, flowers)
- Personal care items (opened cosmetics, hygiene products)
- Custom or personalized orders
- Items marked as **Final Sale**

For questions, contact our support team at **support@shopsmart.com** or call **1-800-SHOP-SMART**.
